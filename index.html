Felicia Workspace

A personal productivity and calendar management workspace powered by Hermes.

About

Felicia Workspace is a privately operated assistant used to organize schedules, coordinate appointments, and support personal and professional workflows. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Calendar Integration

With authorization, Hermes can access Google Calendar to retrieve events, prepare daily schedule briefings, and create, update, or delete calendar events when requested by the authorized user.

How It Works

Automated daily briefings are generated using local software and delivered through Discord. Conversational calendar assistance may use OpenAI services to process scheduling requests and related calendar information.

Privacy

Learn how Google Calendar information is accessed, processed, and stored in our Privacy Policy.
